commit `27eac0d0f8ceab714cdf7640d50f4c4d57ed7f3d`.

Benoit Blanchon ArduinoJson. The inherited display bitmap artwork is credited
upstream to sbrin/lopaka. Original FastLED use is replaced by the safety boundary.
